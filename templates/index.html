📖 What does this mean in simple words?

Why is Avg Price negative? The model found that in this dataset, as average price per unit goes up, fewer units are sold, so total sales revenue goes down. This is classic price-demand behavior — higher price → less demand. The coefficient is negative because it reduces the final prediction.
